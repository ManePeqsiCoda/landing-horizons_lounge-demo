## 2. design.md

### Visual Identity: Horizons Sunset Luxury

#### Aesthetic Direction
- **Photography-First:** Images and videos must occupy 100% of the viewport.
- **Typography:** Sophisticated hierarchy using three distinct fonts:
  - **Headings:** High-contrast Serif (e.g., Playfair Display) for an "expensive" feel.
  - **Secondary/Subheadings:** Elegant, spaced Sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat).
  - **Body:** Clean, legible modern font with generous letter-spacing.
- **Menu Style:** Sticky navigation appearing after the first scroll. Large text (bigger than Zara’s), no background, high contrast for maximum legibility over photography.

#### Color Palette (The Sunset Ritual)
- **Primary:** Signature Yellow (`#FFD700`) from the logo for accents.
- **Accents:** Deep Oranges, Soft Corals, and Ocean Blues.
- **Surface:** Refined Dark Mode backgrounds or frosted glass (Glassmorphism) to ensure text legibility over busy photos.
